About the Centre

Where ideas become action. Where action becomes impact.

CESS is an interdisciplinary hub that explores the powerful connection between society and the environment.

We combine
Social Work Behavioural Science Sustainability Research Innovation Global Partnerships

Our mission is to develop solutions that are not only innovative in theory, but practical in communities and meaningful for future generations.

We believe the most powerful change happens when knowledge crosses disciplines, cultures, and borders.

Vision
To be a global leader in fostering resilient, inclusive societies through innovative ecological stewardship and transformative social action research, recognized as a centre of excellence in sustainability science and practice.
Mission
To design, deliver, and evaluate evidence-based programmes and research that promote ecological justice, public health, social equity, and sustainable community development through a “glocal” (global-local) approach.

Programmes · 2025–2026

Nine programmes across three strategic tiers.

To keep its approach multidimensional, the Centre organises its work into three tiers — direct community intervention, campus-wide engagement, and academic masterclasses that bring global research alongside indigenous knowledge.

TIER 01

Community Outreach & Grassroots Interventions

Programmes directly engaging vulnerable, marginalised or external community populations to drive behavioural change.

Empowering Healthy Ageing

192Beneficiaries
Venues
LR Nagar, Tilak Nagar, and CHRIST University (administrative staff)
Focus
Geriatric care, preventive healthcare, SDG 3
Partner
St. John's Geriatric Centre

What we learned. Theoretical health advice gave way to practical, demonstrative learning — participants benefited most from live physiotherapy and aerobic demonstrations. Sustained community health requires intersectional intervention: nutritional literacy and emotional well-being alongside physical exercise.

Nasha Mukt Bharat (NMBA) Deaddiction Awareness Programme

103Beneficiaries
Venue
St. Patrick's School, Bengaluru
Focus
Substance abuse prevention, peer pressure management
Audience
Students and staff

What we learned. Traditional lecture formats proved insufficient for behavioural change. A role-play enacted by MSW students carried the familial toll of substance abuse far more effectively, establishing peer-to-peer interactive engagement as the method that works in school settings. The programme closed with a unified NMBA pledge.

TIER 02

Campus Awareness & Student Engagement Initiatives

Interactive, university-wide events designed to foster empathy, inclusivity and personal accountability among the student body and staff.

Promoting Healthy Ageing

66Beneficiaries
Venue
Seminar Hall, PU Block, CHRIST University
Date
5 January 2026
Focus
Occupational wellness, active ageing, SDG 3
Partner
St. John's Geriatric Centre (SJGC) — A Ray of Hope

What we learned. A staff wellness initiative giving administrative staff insight into maintaining physical and mental well-being as they age. The takeaway was the need for proactive health intervention within the workplace — institutional sustainability begins with the holistic wellness of an institution's own workforce.

“Second Chance” — Promoting Holistic Well-being

100+Beneficiaries
Venue
CHRIST University campus, SDG Week stall
Focus
SDG 3, SDG 10 and SDG 11
Format
Dumbbell Endurance Challenge, Emoji Emotional Quiz, BMI assessments

What we learned. Gamifying sustainability turned global goals into personal, actionable responsibility for young people. The sale of unused accessories promoted responsible consumption, linking personal lifestyle choices directly to ecological sustainability.

Beyond Vision: Understanding Corneal Blindness & Blind Walk

100Beneficiaries
Venue
Central Block to Block 2, CHRIST University
Focus
Empathy-building, cornea donation awareness
Partner
Sumanahalli Society

What we learned. A paradigm shift from sympathy to empathy. Testimony from a visually impaired student established that financial charity is often less empowering than actionable medical support, and the experiential Blind Walk — navigating the campus without sight — produced a surge of students pledging to donate their corneas.

TIER 03

Academic Masterclasses & Global Perspectives

High-level seminars and international workshops aimed at building intellectual capacity and bridging global research with indigenous knowledge.

International Workshop on Sustainability, Climate Change, and Community

98MSW students
Status
Flagship workshop
Partners
KATHO University, Germany, with local indigenous leaders
Subject
“Aging in Times of Climate Change”, set against indigenous tribal knowledge of forest ecosystems

What we learned. Juxtaposing global research with indigenous knowledge produced a holistic view of sustainability. Ecological conservation is not an isolated academic pursuit but an intersectional necessity that must honour ancestral heritage.

Revisited objectives · 2026–2027

Five core strategic objectives.

The Centre's thrust areas for the coming year, each carrying its own deliverables.

01

Expand community health & wellbeing programmes

  • Scale the Empowering Healthy Ageing initiative to 6 additional community clusters, targeting 400+ elderly participants.
  • Develop longitudinal follow-up mechanisms to document behavioural change outcomes.
  • Formalise an MoU with St. John's Geriatric Centre for sustained partnership.
02

Institutionalize global scholarship & comparative research

  • Establish a CESS Research Lab with dedicated faculty and postgraduate researchers.
  • Launch an international collaborative research project with Katho University, Germany.
  • Publish 3 peer-reviewed articles and 2 book chapters in 2026–2027.
  • Host a biannual CESS Research Seminar with international scholars.
03

Develop revenue-generating academic programmes

  • Launch the certificate course “ESG Implementation for Social Work Professionals”.
  • Develop the micro-credential “2-Week Intensive: Disability Justice & Inclusive Practice”.
  • Establish a paid internship programme for national and international students.
04

Strengthen the school-to-university social sustainability pipeline

  • Develop a multi-year school outreach programme with partner schools.
  • Create youth-led Sustainability Cohorts in partner schools.
  • Document and disseminate school-based intervention models.
05

Strengthen institutional positioning & advocacy

  • Establish the CESS Publications Series: annual working paper and policy briefs.
  • Pursue academic recognition and university-wide integration of CESS modules.
  • Advocate for funding from CSR departments and development organisations.
