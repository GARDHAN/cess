About the Centre

Where ideas become action. Where action becomes impact.

CESS is an interdisciplinary hub that explores the powerful connection between society and the environment.

We combine
Social Work Behavioural Science Sustainability Research Innovation Global Partnerships

Our mission is to develop solutions that are not only innovative in theory, but practical in communities and meaningful for future generations.

We believe the most powerful change happens when knowledge crosses disciplines, cultures, and borders.
